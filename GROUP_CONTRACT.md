## 2. Roles

Suggested roles: coordinator (keeps the plan and the issue list), designer (docs/design.md and the
hand trace), implementer (src/a1_triage.py), tester (tests and ruff), reviewer (reads every change
before it is merged). Everybody writes code and everybody commits. Roles rotate after the first
two weeks. After the first two weeks of the assignment cycle, roles rotate across members in the next weekly check-in so everyone gains experience across coordination, design, implementation, testing, and review while keeping core deliverables aligned.

## 3. How we make decisions

We discuss options in our group chat and check-ins, aim for consensus first, and if we cannot agree we take a majority vote with the coordinator breaking any tie. Key decisions on tasks, scope, and deliverables are recorded in our group chat notes and reflected in `README.md`, `docs/design.md`, and commit messages.

## 4. Meetings and communication

We hold our check-ins weekly (and more frequently close to deadlines) via our group chat channel (WhatsApp) and coordinate file updates in GitHub/VS Code. Group members aim to reply within 24 hours on normal days and within a few hours when approaching a milestone or deadline.

## 5. Rules for Git and for code

- Every member works under their own name: `git config user.name` and `user.email` are set on every machine.
- Every member makes at least 3 commits of real work. Nobody commits for somebody else.
- Commit messages say what changed and why. Small commits are better than one big commit.
- Changes are reviewed by at least one teammate before merging or finalizing on `main`, and `data/variants.csv`, `data/params.json`, and `ruff.toml` are never modified.

## 6. If someone does not contribute

If a member falls behind or does not contribute as agreed, we first send a friendly reminder after one week, then hold a group talk to resolve blockers and redistribute immediate support if needed, and if the issue continues we send an email to the lecturer. The lecturer will use the Git commit history and the individual Q&A to adjust marks fairly.

## 7. Use of AI tools (see the Assignment 1 brief)

We keep ONE shared AI_USE_LOG.md. Each member logs their own AI interactions (including date, tool, prompt, output summary, verification, edits made, and where used) as they work, and we cross-check all entries together in `AI_USE_LOG.md` before final submission to make sure it is complete and accurate. Every member is responsible for the whole submission, including AI use by others, and
every member signs the log and this contract.
